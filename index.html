Our story

How it began

Goal Diggers began in Kigali as a small circle of friends who wanted to do more than talk about books. From the first meeting — where we read together and imagined what a generous life might look like — the idea took shape: what if reading well and giving well could live in the same room?

Today we're a growing community of readers in Kigali, meeting once a month around a chosen book, and paying school fees for five remarkable children whose futures we get to be part of. Every member is a reader. Every reader is a sponsor.
